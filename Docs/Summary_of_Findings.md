# Titanic Dataset – Summary of Findings

## Introduction
This analysis explores passenger survival in the Titanic training dataset, comparing survival rates by sex, passenger class, and age group.

## Summary statistics
The dataset contains **891 passengers** and **12 original variables**. The overall survival rate is **38.4%** (342 survivors). Mean passenger age is **29.7 years** and the median is **28 years**. Age is missing for **177** passengers and Cabin is missing for **687** passengers.

## Key findings

**By sex:** female passengers had a survival rate of **74.2%**, compared with **18.9%** for male passengers.

**By passenger class:** first-class survival was **63.0%**, second-class was **47.3%**, and third-class was **24.2%**.

**By age group:** children had the highest survival rate (**58.0%**). Teenagers had **42.9%**, young adults **38.3%**, adults **40.0%**, and seniors **22.7%**. Passengers with missing ages are excluded from this comparison.

## Conclusion
In this dataset, survival was associated with sex, passenger class, and age group. Female passengers, first-class passengers, and children had higher observed survival rates than their comparison groups. These descriptive associations do not establish causation.
